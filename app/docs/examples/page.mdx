# LaTeX examples

## Inline formulas

The complex amplitude of a wave is $E(x,y) = A(x,y) e^{i\phi(x,y)}$, where $A$ is the
amplitude and $\phi$ the phase.

Sets: $x \in \R$, $z \in \C$, $n \in \N$, $k \in \Z$.

## Display formulas

The Fourier transform:

$$
\mathcal{F}\{f(x)\} = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(x) e^{-2\pi i \xi x} \, dx
$$

Fresnel diffraction:

$$
U(x', y') = \frac{e^{ikz}}{i\lambda z} \iint U(x, y) \exp\left[\frac{ik}{2z}\left((x'-x)^2 + (y'-y)^2\right)\right] dx \, dy
$$

## Equations

The Helmholtz equation:

$$
\nabla^2 U + k^2 U = 0
$$

where $k = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda}$ is the wavenumber.

## Matrices

The ray-transfer matrix of a thin lens:

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 \\
-\frac{1}{f} & 1
\end{pmatrix}
$$

## Alignment

$$
\begin{aligned}
E_{out} &= t(x,y) \cdot E_{in} \\
&= |t| e^{i\phi_t} \cdot A_{in} e^{i\phi_{in}} \\
&= |t| A_{in} e^{i(\phi_t + \phi_{in})}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Fractions and subscripts

The angular spectrum transfer function:

$$
H(f_x, f_y) = \exp\left(ikz\sqrt{1 - \lambda^2 f_x^2 - \lambda^2 f_y^2}\right)
$$

The point spread function:

$$
I(x,y) = \left|\mathcal{F}^{-1}\{H \cdot \mathcal{F}\{U_0\}\}\right|^2
$$
